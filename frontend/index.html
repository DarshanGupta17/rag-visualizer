The SQL that runs 
SELECT id, chunk_id, content, 1 - (embedding <=> :query_vector) AS similarity
FROM chunks
ORDER BY embedding <=> :query_vector
LIMIT :top_k

<=> is pgvector's cosine distance operator. Distance is 1 − similarity, so ordering by smallest distance returns the most similar chunks.

6

Results

A higher cosine similarity means the two vectors point in a closer direction, so the texts are usually closer in meaning.

Search results appear here, ranked by similarity.

How cosine similarity works

cos(θ) = A · B‖A‖ ‖B‖

It measures the angle between two vectors and ignores their length. 1 means the same direction, 0 means unrelated, negative means opposite.

BGE vectors are normalized to length 1, so the score is simply the dot product of the two 384-number lists.

Compare scores against each other rather than reading them as percentages.
